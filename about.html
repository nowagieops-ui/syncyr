Founder of SYNCYR® and CEYMIRION®

Dr Bindunie
Sirimanne Rowland

MD FRSM

Dr Bindunie Sirimanne Rowland is a British entrepreneur, AI scientist and inventor, and a physician by training. Born in Sri Lanka, she is the founder of SYNCYR, CEYMIRION and WellBeology, bringing a polymath approach to work across science, technology, business and the arts.

At CEYMIRION and SYNCYR, she leads research, invention and architectural development focused on behavioural continuity, accountability and human agency in AI. She originated Relational Attractor Yoking (R.A.Y.), a proposed framework for studying interaction-driven behavioural stability in generative systems. Her work includes nine UK patent applications and a six-paper research programme. She directs the translation of this work into practical systems, working with engineers and scientific advisers on development and evaluation.

Her background combines service as a military Captain and Medical Officer with executive leadership, board and advisory appointments, wellness entrepreneurship, international performance and philanthropy. Multilingual and experienced across disciplines and cultures, she brings scientific, commercial and creative perspectives to company leadership, international partnerships and the development of technology centred on people.
